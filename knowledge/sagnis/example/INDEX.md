# The example workspace — mirror index of `example/`

Hand-maintained. Everything here is fictional and was generated by the package's own
scaffold at tier 2, then filled by hand. Paths inside it read `~/.claude` and
`~/Projects/Fernwood Landscaping` as they would on a real machine.
