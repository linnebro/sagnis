A quote is one page: scope, one price, and a "not included" list. Never an hourly breakdown, never a range. Dates are written in words ("the week of April 6").

**Why:** a 2026-09-08 quote with hours listed turned into a negotiation over the hours instead of the work. Maya: "they buy the patio, not the afternoon."

**How to apply:** every quote is filled from Templates/quote.md. A change to the format goes in the template first, then into any quote still open; never in one quote alone.
