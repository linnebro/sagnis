## Mowing

Weekly mowing on a set day: north side Mondays, river road Wednesdays, town Fridays.
One price a month, no per-visit surprises. A rain day rolls to Saturday, and you hear
from us before it does. Edging, trimming and blowing off the walk are part of every
visit, not extras.

## Spring and fall cleanups

One visit that gets the beds, the leaves and the winter debris off the property and
into the truck. Booked by route so the crew is already on your street.

## Patios, walkways and walls

Paver patios, flagstone walkways, and retaining walls up to four feet. Every job
starts with a site visit and ends with one written price for the whole job. No
hourly rates, no allowances, no change orders for things we should have measured.

Use the form below or call. Site visits for hardscape are Tuesday and Thursday
afternoons; you will have a written quote the same evening.
