Ridgeline Supply delivers bulk mulch and patio stone. Orders placed by Thursday noon
arrive Monday; the delivery minimum is three yards, so small jobs are batched. Contact
details and account number live in `Customers/suppliers.csv` (gitignored), never in a
memory file or a quote.
