A hardscape quote is not written until the site visit has recorded: base depth,
where water goes, whether the skid steer fits through the gate (minimum 36 inches),
and the stone lead time from [[reference_supplier_ridgeline]]. The checklist is
`Templates/site-visit.md`; a quote drafted without it is redone, and two were.
