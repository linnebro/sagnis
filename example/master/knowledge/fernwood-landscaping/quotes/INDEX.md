# Quotes — mirror index of `~/Projects/Fernwood Landscaping/Quotes/`

Hand-maintained. A job folder is `YYYY-MM-<customer-slug>/` with `site-visit.md`
(the filled checklist) and `quote.md` (filled from `Templates/quote.md`). Maya sends
the quote herself. A folder suffix marks status: none = open, `-won`, `-lost`,
`-expired`.

## Folders
- `2026-09-hollis-patio/` — 14 by 18 patio, site visit 2026-09-10, quote sent by Maya that evening, open
- `2026-09-ferris-wall-lost/` — retaining wall; quoted 2026-09-03 without the checklist and with hours listed, redone 2026-09-10 after a proper site visit, lost on price
- `2026-08-marlow-walkway-won/` — flagstone walkway, done 2026-08-28; the reference job for walkway quotes

## Rules for this folder
- No quote without `site-visit.md` filled ([[reference_hardscape_quote_checklist]]).
- A format change goes in `Templates/quote.md` first, then into any open quote.
