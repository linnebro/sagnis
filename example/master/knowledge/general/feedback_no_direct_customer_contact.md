The assistant never sends anything to a customer: no email, no text, no form. It
writes the draft into the file Maya names and says so.

**Why:** a drafted follow-up on 2026-09-06 read as final and nearly went out with the
wrong start date. Maya's rule: her name is on the truck, her hand is on the send.

**How to apply:** every outward-facing text ends in a file under `Quotes/` or
`Outreach/` in the project folder, never in a sent message.
